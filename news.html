Event Sponsorship June 2026

Fenix Capital Sponsors the Allied Innovation Summit

Fenix Capital is proud to announce that it is hosting the Allied Innovation Summit, sponsored by Winston Taylor LLP. The Summit brings together allied-nation defense innovators, procurement officials, and early-stage technology founders to accelerate the adoption of critical infrastructure software across NATO and Five Eyes partner nations.

The Allied Innovation Summit convenes the decision-makers and builders shaping the next generation of defense and industrial technology — exactly the ecosystem Fenix Capital was built to serve. Our partnership with Winston Taylor LLP reflects our commitment to being inside the room where allied industrial coordination takes shape.
