Investment Thesis

Software is the new arsenal.

The defense supply chain is undergoing a generational shift. Decades of fragmentation, analog workflows, and single-source dependencies have created a critical vulnerability — one that software can fix.


Fenix Capital backs pre-seed founders building the software layer that makes the US and allied defense industrial base faster, more transparent, and more resilient.


We write first checks into companies solving real procurement, compliance, and coordination problems — before they're obvious.

01
Supply Chain Visibility
Software that gives DoD buyers and prime contractors real-time insight into Tier 2/3 supplier health, capacity, and risk.
02
Compliance & Regulatory Tech
Tools that make CMMC, ITAR, FAR/DFARS, and export control manageable for suppliers who can't afford dedicated compliance teams.
03
Supply Chain Resilience
Platforms that model, stress-test, and diversify critical material and component dependencies across the defense industrial base.
04
Allied Industrial Coordination
Software enabling trusted US and allied partners — Five Eyes, NATO, AUKUS — to coordinate production and procurement at speed.
