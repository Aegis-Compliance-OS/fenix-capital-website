Dana Mincey-Breaux
Managing Director

Dana Mincey-Breaux is the Managing Director of Fenix Capital, a Washington, DC-based pre-seed venture fund investing in defense supply chain, dual-use, logistics, and RegTech software across the U.S. and NATO-allied nations. A U.S. Army veteran with active TS/SCI clearance, she brings firsthand operational experience from her advisory work with the Defense Logistics Agency, where she connected prime contractors to emerging suppliers across the defense industrial base. She founded Fenix on the conviction that technology is the new arsenal — and that the best defense investments come from operators who know the terrain.

How We Work

We take one board seat per investment and stay deeply engaged through the critical early milestones — first contract, first enterprise customer, first regulatory hurdle. We don't write checks and disappear.

→ Introductions to government procurement offices, prime contractors, and enterprise buyers across our network
→ Regulatory readiness support — compliance, licensing, and certification — through our advisor network
→ Help structuring first contracts, pricing models, and go-to-market strategy
→ Warm introductions to allied-nation partners, investment agencies, and institutional customers across 24+ countries
